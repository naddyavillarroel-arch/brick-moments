# Privacy Policy for Brick Moments

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the **Brick Moments** application (package: `com.namay.brickmoments`) handles your information. 

**Developer Contact:** naddya.villarroel@gmail.com

---

### Summary
Brick Moments runs locally on your phone and **does not collect, send, or share your data** with us or third parties. There are no user accounts, no advertisements, and no analytics or tracking tools implemented.

### What Information the App Uses
Everything you enter (photos sets and minifigures, descriptions, and notes) is stored **exclusively in your device's local storage**. We have no access to your data because it never leaves your phone to any external server.

### Permissions Requested
* **Camera:** Used strictly when you choose to take a new photo within the app to photograph a set or minifigure.
* **Photos & Media Gallery:** Used to pick an existing image from your gallery or, if enabled, to save a copy of photos taken within the app to a "Brick Moments" album in your gallery. *(This gallery copy is optional and can be turned off in Settings).*

The app **does not** request or access your location, microphone, contacts, or advertising ID.

### Photos
Photos you capture or select are stored in the app's private storage area. If gallery saving is enabled, a copy is also placed in your device gallery. Once saved to your gallery, those files are governed by your device's standard media policies and any cloud backup services you have active (such as Google Photos).

### Backups (Export & Import)
* **Export Backup:** Generates a file containing your registered collection information and opens Android's share menu so you can decide where to store or send it (e.g., Google Drive, local storage, or email). What happens to that exported file depends entirely on the storage service you select and its privacy policy.
* **Import Backup:** Only reads the specific backup file you select from your device storage.

*Note: Android's native system backup may automatically save app data to your personal Google Account depending on your device settings. That process is managed directly by Google.*

### Ads, Analytics & Third Parties
* **No Ads:** The app is 100% ad-free.
* **No Tracking:** No third-party SDKs, analytics, or behavioral tracking scripts are included.
* **No Data Sharing:** We do not sell or share any user data.

### Data Retention & Deletion
Your data remains on your device until you decide to delete it. You can manually delete any moment or favorite entry directly within the app. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data stored in the app's private directory. *(Photos exported to your public gallery or manually generated backup files must be deleted separately).*

### Security
Your data is kept in the app's protected space on your device. Security depends on your device's security practices (such as screen locks and system updates).

### Children's Privacy
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

### Changes to This Policy
If we update how the application handles information, this policy and the "Last updated" date will be updated accordingly.
